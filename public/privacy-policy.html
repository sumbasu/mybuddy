Privacy Policy

How SweatBud collects, uses, and protects the information you share when you're finding your next game.

01Overview

SweatBud ("SweatBud", "we", "us") helps people find local partners for tennis, badminton, cricket, pickleball, and other racket and team sports. This policy explains what information the SweatBud app collects when you sign up, build a profile, and connect with other players — and what we do, and don't do, with it.

It applies to the SweatBud mobile app on iOS and Android. By creating an account, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

02Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what's generated as you use the app, and what device permissions you choose to grant.

Account & profile

Data tied to your SweatBud profile
FieldWhy we ask
Phone number or Google accountSigning in and verifying it's really you
Name, age, genderShown on your profile; used for buddy-preference matching
City & stateFinding games and players near you
Sport interests, skill preferencesSuggesting relevant games and partners

Activity on SweatBud

  • Games and activities you create, join, or request to join
  • Posts you share in the Community feed, and who you follow
  • Ratings and reviews you give or receive after playing
  • Messages you send other players through in-app chat

Device permissions (only if you grant them)

  • Location — to show nearby games and improve match suggestions
  • Camera & photo library — to set a profile photo or share one in a post
  • Microphone — only if you record or send a voice note in chat
  • Push notifications — to alert you about join requests, messages, and upcoming games

You can decline any of these when prompted, or change them later in your device settings. Declining is fine — SweatBud just won't be able to offer that specific feature (e.g. nearby-game suggestions without location).

What we don't collect We don't buy contact lists or data from data brokers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites for advertising.

03How we use it

  • Operate your account and verify your identity at sign-in
  • Match you with nearby players and games that fit your interests
  • Show your profile, posts, and activity to other users as intended by the app's features
  • Send you notifications about requests, matches, and messages
  • Process subscription payments and manage your free trial
  • Investigate reports of abuse and enforce our Terms & Conditions
  • Improve app reliability and fix bugs

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your activity data to build advertising profiles.

04Sharing & third parties

SweatBud is built on a small number of trusted infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf, under their own security and confidentiality commitments:

ProviderHandles
Google FirebaseAccount sign-in, app database, photo storage
Google Sign-InOptional one-tap login, if you choose it
RazorpaySubscription payment processing — SweatBud never sees or stores your card details

Your name, city, sport interests, and public posts are visible to other SweatBud users by design — that's how the app helps you find people to play with. Your phone number and exact location are never shown to other users.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of our users, or in connection with a merger or acquisition of SweatBud — in which case we'd tell you first.

05Retention & deletion

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from Settings, or by writing to us, we delete your profile, posts, and activity history within 30 days, except where we're required to retain records for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Ratings you've given or received may be retained in aggregated, de-identified form to preserve the integrity of other users' rating history.

06Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and edit your profile information directly in the app
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Withdraw a previously granted device permission via your phone's settings

To exercise any of these, reach us at the email in Contact us.

07Age requirement

SweatBud is for users aged 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

08Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections, access controls, and Firebase's security infrastructure — to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute protection, but we work to keep your data safe and will notify affected users if a breach puts personal information at meaningful risk.

09International transfers

SweatBud's infrastructure providers operate global data centers. Your information may be processed outside your home country, including in the United States, under those providers' standard data-protection commitments.

10Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your information, we'll notify you in the app before it takes effect. The "Effective" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

11Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at support@sweatbud.com.
